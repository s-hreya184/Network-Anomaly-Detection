HOW IT WORKS

A simple, 4-step look at how the AI spots suspicious activity and protects your network.

01

1. Observe Traffic

Every internet connection works like a phone call. The system looks at basic call details: how long it lasted, how fast data traveled, and how much information went back and forth.

02

2. Prepare the Data

Computers understand numbers best. Words like "Web Browsing" or "File Transfer" are turned into numerical scores so the AI model can compare them fairly and quickly.

03

3. AI Safety Check

A committee of 100 trained AI decision-makers reviews the conversation. They compare it against thousands of known patterns to decide if it's normal everyday browsing or an attack.

04

4. Clear Explanation

Instead of just giving a warning, the system tells you in plain English why it looked suspicious and provides steps to block it.
